# Playable Spaces brand (Instagram @playablespaces)

The look is Anas's locked brand (`Guidelines/locked_brand_guidelines.md`): warm paper ground with a soft glow and a faint grid, ink type, green for the things, orange for the one thing to look at, Newsreader heavy italic plus Montserrat 800 plus Inter. The wordmark is the one on the site: *Playable* in Newsreader italic 800, **SPACES** in Montserrat 800 green.

Everything here is drawn in code in `brand/kit.html` and exported by `node tools/export-brand.mjs` to `brand/out/<id>.png` (1080 x 1080).

## Reel covers and the grid

1. Every reel cover is the whole home on its plinth, from the hook's last frame (furniture landed, ticker done), so the grid reads as a row of little homes.
2. One line of title on the cover, in the wordmark style: Newsreader italic for the lead, Montserrat green for the brand name, e.g. *Habitt* **HOME**.
3. Captions in the reels use the locked brand: Newsreader heavy italic, key words in orange.
