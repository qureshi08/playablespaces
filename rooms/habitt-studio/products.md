## Modelling status (2026-10-22)

All 9 are modelled in `products/habitt/`, each built to its listed size (checked on `kit/turntable.html`, every built size within 1.5 cm of the listing except the armchair, see below) and compared with the store photo for at most two rounds. Colours: the Billy sofa has all four jute colours and the armchair all three frame colours ready in code; the swap button comes with the product cards.

## Things to check before modelling

1. **Lighting fails the size rule.** None of Habitt's table lamps list a size, and its lanterns list only a height. Until Habitt gives sizes, the lamps in this space stay generic (no Habitt name, no buy link).
2. **Cane Charm Armchair depth is listed as 16.5 inches**, which is shallow for an armchair. Model it as listed and compare against the photos; if the photos clearly disagree, ask Habitt rather than change the number.
3. **Only three pieces come in colours** (sofa, armchair, and black only for the listed chair), so restyling in this space is mostly walls, floor and rugs, with the sofa and armchair colours as the product swaps.
4. Prices on the site are sale prices and change; every product card shows the date the price was checked.

## Rejected and why

0. Arlen Study Table (picked first, swapped on 2026-10-22 while modelling): its photos show a black desk with a tall shelf unit, but the listing says oak veneer and only 30 inches high, and the shelf unit height is not listed anywhere.

1. Jack Floor Bed: its king size is listed as 85.2 W × 73.2 D while its own recommended mattress is 78 × 72, so the listing contradicts itself.
2. Hendrick Study Table: listed at 24 inches high, too low to be a desk.
3. Linna 4 Person Dining Table: an 11 inch high floor table, not a fit for this flat.
4. Rovak 2 seater (5 colours, 57.5 × 22 × 35 in, Rs 75,905): a good second sofa if more colour choice matters more than price.
5. Every table lamp: no size listed.
