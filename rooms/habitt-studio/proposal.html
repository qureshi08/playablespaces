Habitt space, take three

A smaller, fuller flat built around Habitt's nine pieces. Every colour except the products' own comes from one Wada combination, including the floor, the frames, the plinth and the surroundings. Nothing is built until you pick.

1. The references

Real lived-in homes from Unsplash (Pinterest needs your login, Apartment Therapy now blocks automated reading). Each one gives one thing.

2. The room

8.0 × 4.8 m, 38 m². Half the old floor, so nothing floats in space (the old one was 58 m²).
Bedroom behind a black framed glass partition, the bed under its own window with a lamp each side (refs 29, 37).
Living packed together: sofa backed to the glass with a side table and lamp at each end, Kade on the rug, two cane chairs facing (refs 13, 4).
Dining by the window, a kitchen run along the left wall, shelves of plants and prints above the OTTO shelf (refs 3, 1).
Dressed like a home: cushions and a throw on the sofa, books and a tray on Kade, plants in every corner, prints, curtains, a pendant over the table.
Work corner in the bedroom by the right wall.

3. Pick a palette

Each card is one Wada combination applied to everything that is not a Habitt product. Strong colours only go on small things. I would use all three as the space's three moods, with your pick as the one it opens on.
