A moodboard that builds the room

Designers pin samples to a board and picture the room. Here the samples go straight into the room: a flat cut open on a plinth, with a living room, a dining corner, a bedroom and a study. Change the materials, move the furniture and pull the sun around, and watch how the room feels.

Samples

Drag a sample from the tray onto a surface. Wood goes on the floor, the slat wall and the woodwork; stone on the floor and the walls; paint on the walls and the slats; fabric on the sofa and chairs, the cushions, the bed and the rugs. Hold it over a surface to see a patch of it in place, and let go to spread it. Or click a surface first, then a sample.

Furniture

Drag a piece from the Furniture tab into the room, or pick up anything already there. Pieces snap to a 10 cm grid and to the walls, and won't go through each other or the partitions. Scroll while holding one to turn it. Click a piece to turn it or take it out.

Light

Drag the sun along its arc, or use the Daylight slider. In the morning it comes in low through the dining window on the left. Around midday it sits behind the flat and pours through the big window. In the evening it comes in low and gold over the open side, and after dark the lamps take over. The four previews show your flat at four times of day.

Cozy, bright or moody

The meter reads the room as you change it. Bright comes from pale surfaces and daylight. Cozy comes from warm colours, soft textures and low gold light. Moody comes from dark, deep surfaces and pools of lamplight. Most rooms are a mix.
